Frequently Asked Questions – SEO Pro
 
1. What is SEO Pro?
SEO Pro is an all-in-one SEO SaaS platform you host on your own server. It includes keyword research, competitor research, site audits, rank tracking, backlinks, local SEO, an AI article writer, AI chat and AI search visibility. Your customers buy plans and spend credits on lookups.

2. What technology is it built with?
PHP 8.3, Laravel 13, Livewire 4 and Tailwind CSS 4, with MySQL 8 or MariaDB 10.11+. It can also be installed as a PWA on desktop and mobile.

3. Do I get the full source code?
Yes. You receive the complete source code with no encryption and no domain limitations.

4. Where does the SEO data come from?
Search data comes from the DataForSEO API: Google Organic SERP, DataForSEO Labs, Backlinks and Google Maps SERP. Site Audit uses the built-in crawler and does not need DataForSEO.

5. Are the DataForSEO and AI API costs included in the price?
No. You connect your own DataForSEO account and your own AI provider keys, and each provider bills your account for usage. DataForSEO is pay-as-you-go, and lookups are cached for 24 hours to reduce cost.

6. Which AI providers are supported?
Anthropic Claude, OpenAI ChatGPT, Google Gemini and Perplexity. You need at least one key for the AI features. You choose a default provider and can change the model name for each one in the admin panel.

7. What is AI Search Visibility?
It asks every connected AI assistant the same prompts, with web search turned on, and compares the answers. You see how brands are mentioned, the sentiment, which sources are cited, and where the opportunities are. Connecting all four providers gives the most complete picture.

8. How does the credit system work?
Each paid action costs credits, for example 10 for a site audit, 1 for keyword research and 15 for an AI article. The admin can change every cost. A paid lookup is charged once per team, feature and day, and failed lookups are refunded automatically.

9. Which payment gateways are supported?
Stripe, Razorpay, PayPal and bank transfer. Bank transfer payments are approved manually by an admin. You can enable one or more gateways.

10. Do I need to edit files to add API keys?
No. Data, AI and payment keys are entered under Admin ? Settings and stored encrypted. Only email (SMTP) is configured in the .env file.

11. Can I test it without spending money on API calls?
Yes. DataForSEO has a sandbox mode with free sample data, and the payment gateways work with sandbox or test keys. The sample numbers are not real, so turn sandbox mode off before going live.

12. Does it support teams and white-label reports?
Yes. Users can invite team members who share the team’s credits, and reports can carry the customer’s own branding for agencies.

13. What are the server requirements?
PHP 8.3 with Composer, Node.js and npm to build assets, MySQL 8 or MariaDB 10.11+, an SMTP account, and the ability to run a queue worker and the Laravel scheduler. The worker handles site audits and manual rank updates, and the scheduler runs daily rank checks, AI visibility checks and credit refills.

14. Is installation included?
No free installation service is included. Installation is charged at $5 to $10, depending on the software’s complexity and criticality. An installation guide is provided with the source code.

15. What support and updates do I get?
Unlimited email support and free updates for one year.

16. Is this a one-time payment?
Yes. After full payment you receive the complete source code, and no further payments are required.

17. Can I resell the script?
No. The regular license allows use in a single project, for yourself or a client. Reselling the item or distributing the source files is not allowed. You are free to sell subscriptions to your own hosted SEO Pro site.

18. How soon will I receive the source code?
The download link is sent within 2 to 4 hours on working days (Monday to Friday) and within 4 to 8 hours on weekends.
